This release adds a reading-stats widget, gives you more control over language and device-specific reading settings, and makes it easier to back up your library and take books offline.

### Reading
- Add a home-screen stats card alongside the current-book widget. It shows your current title and progress, plus reading time for this week, month, and year; tap to open the book or reading dashboard. A year of synced reading data stays available offline.
- Choose Liseur’s language independently of your phone, including Simplified Chinese. On Android 13 and newer, the choice also appears in system per-app language settings.
- Recover a saved furthest reading position after syncing, even when offline or reading in continuous-scroll mode. Moving elsewhere before accepting the recovery cancels the offer.
- Keep reading settings such as font, theme, and margins separate on each device signed in to the same account.

### Library
- Back up and restore settings, custom fonts, annotations, and reading positions in a ZIP archive.
- Export books for offline reading.

### Fixes
- Duplicate book or category entries no longer crash library grids.
- Metadata repairs preserve the author names from your EPUB and keep the book’s saved reading place and sync identity.
